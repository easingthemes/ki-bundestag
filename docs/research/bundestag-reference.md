# How the Bundestag Works

> **Doc Status**: Active reference (domain model)
> **Use for**: Parliamentary process rules and action semantics

1. Core Constitutional Framework

The Deutscher Bundestag is Germany’s federal parliament.
It is elected every 4 years and is the central legislative body of the Germany.

Main responsibilities:
•	Pass federal laws
•	Elect the Federal Chancellor
•	Control the Federal Government
•	Approve the federal budget
•	Participate in EU affairs
•	Decide on military deployments

⸻

ALL BODIES IN THE BUNDESTAG SYSTEM

Grouped by category.

⸻

A. Parliamentary Bodies (Internal Organs of the Bundestag)

1. Plenary (Plenum)
   •	All Members of Parliament (MPs)
   •	Main decision-making body

2. President of the Bundestag
   •	Presides over sessions
   •	Maintains order
   •	Represents parliament externally

3. Presidium
   •	President + Vice Presidents
   •	Organizes parliamentary work

4. Council of Elders (Ältestenrat)
   •	Coordinates agenda
   •	Manages parliamentary schedule

5. Parliamentary Committees

Examples:
•	Budget Committee
•	Foreign Affairs Committee
•	Legal Affairs Committee
•	Defense Committee
•	EU Affairs Committee
•	Petition Committee

Special types:
•	Standing Committees
•	Investigative Committees
•	Mediation Committee (with Bundesrat)

⸻

B. Political Actors

1. Political Parties

Examples:
•	Sozialdemokratische Partei Deutschlands (SPD)
•	Christlich Demokratische Union Deutschlands (CDU)
•	Bündnis 90/Die Grünen
•	Freie Demokratische Partei
•	Alternative für Deutschland
•	Die Linke

2. Parliamentary Groups (Fraktionen)
   •	Formal party groups inside Bundestag
   •	Minimum 5% of members required

3. Members of the Bundestag (MdB)
   •	Individual MPs
   •	Direct mandate or party list

⸻

C. Federal Government (Interacts with Bundestag)

1. Federal Chancellor
   •	Elected by Bundestag

2. Federal Ministers
   •	Heads of ministries
   •	Can introduce bills

3. Federal Cabinet
   •	Chancellor + Ministers

⸻

D. External Constitutional Bodies

1. Bundesrat
   •	Represents German states (Länder)
   •	Must approve certain laws

2. Bundespräsident
   •	Signs laws into force
   •	Can refuse signing if unconstitutional

3. Federal Constitutional Court
   •	Reviews constitutionality

3. Plenary (Bundestag as a whole)
   •	Elect Federal Chancellor
   •	Conduct vote of no confidence

   •	Reject law
   •	Amend law
   •	Approve federal budget
   •	Decide military deployment
   •	Establish committees

   •	Suspend member

4. Committees
   •	Draft legislative recommendations
   •	Amend bills
   •	Conduct hearings
   •	Summon experts
   •	Question ministers
   •	Initiate investigative reports
   •	Monitor ministries
   •	Request documents

⸻

5. President of the Bundestag
   •	Open/close session
   •	Maintain order
   •	Call speaker to order
   •	Impose fine
   •	Expel member from sitting
   •	Determine speaking order
   •	Certify adopted laws

⸻

6. Federal Government
   •	Introduce government bill
   •	Issue policy statement
   •	Answer parliamentary questions
   •	Request vote of confidence (Chancellor)
   •	Draft budget
   •	Issue ordinances (if authorized)
   •	Negotiate EU legislation

⸻

7. Bundesrat
   •	Approve consent-required laws
   •	Object to objection laws
   •	Initiate legislation
   •	Request Mediation Committee
   •	Propose amendments

⸻

8. Bundespräsident
   •	Sign law

   •	Dissolve Bundestag (under specific conditions)
   •	Appoint/dismiss Chancellor
